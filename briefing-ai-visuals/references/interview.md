# Interview guide

Ask only what changes what appears on screen. At most three questions per round, most important first. Every question offers two to four labeled options and a default, so "you decide" is a valid answer. Explain the reason in half a sentence.

## Question bank, by priority

| # | Ask | Why (say it) | Options | Default if unanswered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What are you making? | Each job has its own order and risks | Hero image / moving hero / site or app design / product images / brand assets / video | Hero image |
| 2 | What should the viewer do or feel after? | Decides every frame | Understand / try / buy / remember | Understand the offer in one view |
| 3 | Where is it shown, in what format? | Aspect ratio and length change composition and cost | Website desktop and phone / social 9:16 / feed 1:1 or 4:5 / shop or marketplace | Website, 16:9 with a phone crop |
| 4 | What already exists? | A strong asset replaces most of the brief | Site / brand tokens / product photos / logo / footage / nothing | Start from a generated hero |
| 5 | What is the real headline or content? | Placeholder copy gives placeholder layouts | Paste it / write it with me / use a draft | I draft one, marked as draft |
| 6 | Which route, plan and balance? | Credits, uploads and caps differ | Web by hand / agent via MCP or CLI / API | Web for exploring, agent only for locked work |
| 7 | Budget, and what happens at the limit? | The agent has no ceiling of its own | A credit figure / a share of balance | 20% of balance for the first hour, stop at 80% |
| 8 | Must or must not appear? | Prevents blocked or unusable results | Logo / exact text / people / legal | No real faces, no protected characters |
| 9 | A recurring person, product or place? | Decides Soul ID or Elements | Yes, own or consented photos / yes, invented / no | None |
| 10 | Three words for the feeling, and one thing it must not look like | Becomes visible properties | Words / a reference image | Ask for one reference image instead |
| 11 | Who decides it is good, and by what test? | Becomes the keeper checks | Me / a team / a client | Three checks proposed by you |

## How to ask

- Show the understanding first ("I read X and Y, I think it is Z"), then ask.
- Use the structured question tool when the client has one; otherwise a short numbered list with options in brackets.
- If the user answers with a mood ("premium but human"), propose the visible translation and ask yes or no.
- Keep a running list: Known / Assumed / Still open. Show it at hand-over.

## Stop rules

- Stop when the remaining gaps are cheap to fix on a still.
- Stop at once if the user says go. Record open gaps as assumptions and flag the one most likely to cost credits.
- Never interview for the sake of completeness. A precise request may need only a one-line confirmation.
