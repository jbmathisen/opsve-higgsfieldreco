# Interview guide

Ask only what changes what appears on screen. Max three questions per turn, most important first. Every question carries a default, so "you decide" is a valid answer. Explain the reason in half a sentence.

## Contents
- Question bank, by priority
- How to ask
- Stop rules

## Question bank, by priority

| # | Ask | Why (say it) | Default if unanswered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What should the viewer do or feel after? | Decides every shot | Understand the offer in one view |
| 2 | Where is it shown, in what format and length? | Aspect ratio and duration change composition and cost | 16:9, 6 to 8 s, one file |
| 3 | What already exists (site, hero image, logo, footage, moodboard)? | A strong asset replaces most of the brief | Start from a generated hero frame |
| 4 | Which agent and route (web, MCP, API), which plan, what balance? | Credits, uploads and caps differ | Web for exploring, agent only for locked shots |
| 5 | Budget in credits or kronor, and what happens at the limit? | The agent has no ceiling of its own | 20% of balance for the first hour, stop at 80% |
| 6 | Must appear / must not appear (logo, text, people, legal)? | Prevents blocked or unusable results | No real faces, no protected characters |
| 7 | Is there a recurring character or product? Own or consented photos? | Decides Soul ID or Element | None, single shots |
| 8 | Three words for the feeling, and one thing it must not look like | Becomes visible properties in the art direction card | Ask for one reference image instead |
| 9 | Who decides if it is good, and by what test? | Becomes the three yes/no keeper criteria | Three checks proposed by you |

## How to ask

- Show the understanding first ("I read X and Y, I think it is Z"), then ask.
- Offer two or three concrete options instead of an open question when the user is unsure.
- If the user answers with a mood ("premium but human"), do not accept it as is; propose the visible translation and ask yes or no.
- Keep a running list: Known / Assumed / Still open. Show it when you hand over.

## Stop rules

- Stop when the remaining gaps are cheap to fix on a still.
- Stop at once if the user says go. Record the open gaps as assumptions and flag the one most likely to cost credits.
- Never interview for the sake of completeness.
